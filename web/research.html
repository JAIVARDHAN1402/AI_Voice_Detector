The question

A detector says "90 % AI". Can you rely on that when the voice comes from a text-to-speech system it has never seen? For a payment-authorisation system the costly mistake is an AI voice accepted as real.

We test this honestly: leave one whole corpus out of training, calibrate on the others, and count how often the held-out corpus's AI voices get through. Ordinary (split) conformal calibration bounds that rate only for voices like the calibration voices. Domain-robust conformal calibration treats the generator as the exchangeable unit (one threshold per generator, then a low quantile across generators).
